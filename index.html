About Me

The story behind the data

I'm a B.Tech graduate in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science from Vignan's Institute of Management and Technology for Women, Hyderabad (2026). What started as a degree became a genuine curiosity — I fell in love with finding patterns in messy data and presenting them in ways that actually make sense to people.

My approach is simple: understand the problem first, then let the data guide the answer. I've built dashboards analyzing everything from 9,551 restaurants across 15 countries to retail sales worth Rs. 22.9L — each project teaching me something new about how businesses think.

I'm currently working as a Data Analyst intern at two companies simultaneously while looking for full-time Business Analyst or Data Analyst roles in Hyderabad. I believe the best insights are ones that don't need a data background to understand.
